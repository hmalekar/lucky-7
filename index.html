A little luck, explained.

  1. Pick Under 7, Exactly 7, or Over 7.
  2. Enter a bet of 1–9,999 virtual tokens, then roll both dice.
  3. If their total matches your zone, you receive 2× your bet, including your original stake. Otherwise, your stake is lost.
  4. Tap Next player to start a fresh turn.
Office party modeThis game uses adjusted outcomes for office party play. Every zone has the same chance of winning, and each turn is independent.

There is no player wallet; the scorecard records rounds and total tokens returned. Reset clears this browser’s scorecard. This file uses no internet connection or external assets.
